These are options, not quotas, required phases or new schema layout values. Alternate information density: a large short claim gives way to precise evidence, then a result can expand. On 9:16, redesign scale and reading order; do not shrink the square slide into a portrait canvas or fit its entire paragraph beside an image.

## Type as the subject

Write the screen phrase alongside the spoken clause. Use a dominant word, number or sign when it carries the actual distinction. The voice connects it to the example rather than reading a paragraph already displayed. Supporting text should answer the immediate question, not compete at the same weight.

Set Vietnamese line breaks by meaningful phrases. Avoid accidental lone short words, splitting a name or number/unit, compressed diacritics and masks that clip accents. A deliberately isolated word can be the point (source 08); distinguish that from an accidental orphan. Do not force uppercase or overly tight line height to imitate a raster. Reserve expressive scale for the main idea and keep code/captions comfortably readable.

Allow a small overlap between a decorative ribbon and a giant background glyph only if the word stays unambiguous. Keep evidence, important accents and changing values unobscured. Decorative glyphs may crop; teaching glyphs must remain complete.

## Choose the image/animation boundary before generating

1. **Whole composition plate:** A stable hook, metaphor or recap can be generated as one image, including a short exact headline if desired. Describe the reading path, type hierarchy, negative space and final 9:16 format, not just a bag of style adjectives. Verify every word/diacritic and fact in the output. A plate does not make its internal text or objects separately animatable. Avoid using this mode for every scene.
2. **Layered editorial scene:** For a mechanism, generate an art-directed background/context or a subject with blank label areas, plus isolated parts needed for actual changes. Put code, values and cues in native layers that match the image's palette and hierarchy. Plan masks and occlusion up front. This is generally the useful mode for visual reasoning.
3. **Native evidence composition:** Exact comparison, executable code, small changing data and glyph inspection benefit from native text/geometry. Keep the editorial scale/color decisions from the reference; do not reduce the whole video to generic cards. Add a generated subject only when it improves the explanation.

The generation medium is not the style. Native layers must look at home with the generated art; gen-img can also do the overall art direction. Do not impose an all-HTML aesthetic or forbid generated lettering categorically. Conversely, a handsome generated diagram does not establish logical correctness.

## A worked script-design sketch, not a new rendered video

Topic: in JavaScript, `+` can concatenate a string or add numbers. Audience: beginners who know a variable.

- **Question / type-led hook:** “Cùng là 3 và 2. Sao lại ra 32?” Let `32` dominate; connect it to the actual example immediately.
- **Evidence:** Native expression `"3" + 2` and result `"32"`. Emphasize the quotes around the first operand. Voice: the first value is a string, so this example joins text. A generated pair of matte tiles may anchor the operands; exact symbols remain native.
- **One changed condition:** Replace the first operand with the number `3`; show `3 + 2` and `5`. Retain the operand identity and reposition the large result; do not claim the expression changed merely because its color did.
- **Bounded takeaway:** For these two examples, type changes the meaning of `+`. Keep a compact exact before/after pair; no claim that every JavaScript coercion case has been covered.

This offers question → visible distinction → changed outcome → bounded rule. It does not require a literal factory/object journey for every topic. For another concept, change the evidence and narrative rather than recycling this shot sequence.
