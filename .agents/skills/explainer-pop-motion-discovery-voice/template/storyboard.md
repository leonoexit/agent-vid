Chosen familiar format and sources:
Composition reference: approved API-key image-led accent version; transfer its picture-beat approach, not its props or layout.
Field / palette plan: dominant treatment; contrast with actual assets; story role or purely compositional role; motivated changes/returns; repeated series motif (if any).

Opening contract:
- What the first frame lets a newcomer identify, without post context or sound:
- Exact opening scene text / first spoken line:
- Heading accent: emphasized term/question, palette/material, cue and settled state:
- Specific question, contrast or consequence that gives a reason to continue:
- What the image/action contributes, and the next evidence that starts paying off the promise:
Opening through first explanation:

| Spoken claim / source | Subject and visible before-state | Image action → after-state → understanding | Indispensable text and why image alone is ambiguous | Required assets/states: reuse, referenced variant or new | Carriers / cue / handoff / cleared space |

Choose a representation for each passage before HTML layout: photographic cutout, printed lettering, paper label, standalone type or a contextual interface excerpt. Record the library choice, needed variant or new asset. Native geometry needs a specific explanatory role; do not default to SVG props or circular icon badges. A caption/label is a reading or editorial piece, not an app card.

Plan narration and picture beats before HTML layout. Images perform or reveal the explanation; voice connects cause and consequence. Keep text for the hook, names, dates, exact data and concise distinctions. Check each passage without labels: what visibly changes, what carries forward, and what does the voice add? If it is only a sentence plus a decorative picture, restage it. No asset quota; request matching views/states when the action requires them. Captions remain separate and do not count as a substitute for explanatory images.

Give every authored component a carrier and motion treatment, including native objects and standalone type. Group paper with its text and devices with their controls. Vary amplitude/seed; avoid moving all pieces in lockstep or jittering both parent and child. Global viewport and captions stay stable. Material motion does not count as explanatory progress.

Review the first frame and opening seconds muted, without post context, before extending it; record the recognizable subject and reason to continue. Then review with narration. Check hierarchy at phone scale, what each asset adds, whether the type helps orientation, and whether a component was left static merely because it was native HTML/SVG.

Typography roles: heading family / body and explanatory labels / exact technical metadata.

Selected reaction opportunities (if useful): viewer thought → considered visual responses → chosen image/gesture and purpose → entry/exit cue and gaze target → existing asset or new cutout. A simple beat can be left without a reaction when evidence or reading already carries it. Review whether the selected aside adds meaning/personality rather than distraction.
