# Production in this repository

Activate `source scripts/activate.sh`. `<skill>` is `.agents/skills/explainer-pop-motion-discovery-voice`; `<pipeline>` is `.agents/skills/explainer-bento-programming-voice` used only for TTS/timing.

1. `python <skill>/scripts/new-project.py <project>` creates a neutral draft, refusing existing destinations. Author script.json, storyboard.md, sources.md, assets and story.js; the unfinished draft has no playable timeline.
2. Script schema: language vi/en, title, audience, story {question, example, takeaway}, intro/scenes[]/outro each with title/vo and optional cues/hold. Cues are exact phrases or {on, occurrence}; section containers are voice units, not mandatory visual cuts. `python <skill>/scripts/validate-script.py --project <project>` checks the schema only.
3. VI: `python <pipeline>/scripts/tts.py --project <project> --engine vieneu --voice 'Hải Đăng'`. EN uses the pipeline's Kokoro path. Honor stored/explicit preferences. Then `python <pipeline>/scripts/sync-narration.py --project <project> --no-bgm`. Regenerate speech after VO edits. Local word timestamps are estimates; check cue landmarks against actual audio when possible.
4. Preserve root/explainer IDs, AUDIO markers and composition id `pop-collage`. Sync produces SCRIPT/PLAN in project-data.js. Construct after DOMContentLoaded, prebuild semantic states, register one paused timeline in `window.__timelines['pop-collage']`, extend to PLAN.total. No callback-created DOM or wall-clock motion. Captions stay outside moving scene groups.
5. `pop.js` provides node/svg/cue/caption helpers; `collage.js` provides coordinate-explicit image/text/strip/mark/entrance helpers. Project-owned story.js controls causality and editing. Carry an anchor, reveal a detail or use a continuous cover for joins; do not repeat whole-page pushes as the entire motion language.
6. Apply the cold-entry review in story.md to the first frame/opening seconds, then review opening-to-first-payoff and an interior passage with narration, followed by the whole film. Separate editorial judgment from technical checks. Inspect phone legibility, active information order, longest held compositions, joins at ±1 frame, reverse seeks and caption timing. Disclose if only frames/timeline/ASR were available; no approval gate is implied.
7. Run `hyperframes lint <project>` and `hyperframes check <project>`. Fix actual defects. Render with `hyperframes render <project> --quality high --fps 30 --output <project>/renders/<slug>-9x16.mp4`. Inspect metadata, full decode and critical encoded intervals. Deliver MP4, script and source map. Deliver a dedicated Facebook cover alongside the video.

Transitions are silent by default. Do not automatically attach paper swishes, whooshes or noise bursts to cuts or moving paper. Use an SFX only for a specified meaningful action, then review it in the final mix under narration; visual movement does not require a sound. Do not substitute sound for visual development. Silent sync (`--music-only --no-bgm`) is for technical previews only. Do not claim audience validation from render success.

For reusable paper labels, load `backplates.js` before `collage.js`. Inspect text fitting, alpha edges and paper/lettering motion together; see [backplates](backplates.md).

`Collage.paperJitter` supplies deterministic material motion; read [its limits and usage](rosetta-motion.md). Apply it across the authored scene component carriers, including native objects and standalone type, with different amplitudes and seeds. Text/controls travel with their carrier. Do not apply it globally to the camera or captions, or double-apply it to nested members of one assembly. A subtle hold effect does not replace the shot score.
