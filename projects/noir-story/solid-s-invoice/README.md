# S trong SOLID — Noir trial

A 107-second vertical Vietnamese explanation of Single Responsibility Principle through an invoice example.
Voice: Hải Đăng at 1.0, local VieNeu. No music. Created with explainer-noir-story-voice 0.2.0.

- `renders/solid-s-noir-9x16.mp4`: playable final test render (generated/ignored by Git).
- `storyboard.md`: relation, reading task, spatial decision, spoken cue and reference role per beat.
- `index.html`, `project.css`, `story-engine.js`: custom persistent-node choreography.
- `narration.md`, `script.json`, `score.json`: wording, six narrative phases and phrase anchors.
- `facts.md`, `scripts/example.py`, `example-results.json`: content grounding and executable evidence.
- `qa.md`: completed checks, actual media metadata and remaining review limits.

The six narrative phases do not correspond to six preset layouts. Source shapes inspire grouping and hierarchy;
new motion shows ownership changing and independent requests reaching different parts of the code.
